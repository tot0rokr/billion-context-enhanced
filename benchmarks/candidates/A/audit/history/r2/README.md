# Historical R2 artifacts

These reports and findings preserve the results of evidence batch 2 and the R2
recomputation of earlier evidence. R3 superseded their pending-state preservation
across epochs for every batch. Current reports are in `../../`; these archived
results are historical evidence and must not be used as current expected output.
Their original fingerprints are recorded in `../../../PHASE_NOTES.md`.

Operational corrections are separate from event reconstruction. Consult the
current `../../operations-notes.json` for active values and
`../../operations-corrections.json` for explicitly invalidated older values.
